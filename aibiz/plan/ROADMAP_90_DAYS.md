# 90-day plan (week by week)

**Your hours:** about 10–15 hours a week, next to school or studies.

**My part:** I can work only when you open a session and ask. I don't run in the background and I
contact no one. Everything that touches a real person or money is yours: sending, calling, signing,
paying, invoicing.

**Costs** are estimates in EUR. They exclude the €290.98/month contributions that start only if
you register an *obrt* (see the legal section).

in contributions from registration (official 2026 figure). That is why registration should follow,
not precede, paying demand. Check the route with the accountant in week 3.
