# Business website and brand plan (build only after Gate 1)

Don't spend time on branding before a paid pilot. Until then, the one-pager, the demo video and a
simple LinkedIn or Instagram profile are enough.

## Name options

These are checks to run, not recommendations yet. Look each one up in the court register
(sudreg.pravosudje.hr), the crafts register, the .hr domain registry and on Instagram.

| Name | Idea | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upit u termin | Says exactly what it does, in Croatian | Descriptive; hard to trademark |
| Brzi Termin | Short, local | Generic; may already be in use |
| Kvota | Quote → *kvota* | Could be confused with finance |
| Odgovor24 | "Answer 24/7" | Promises availability the owner must still confirm |
| Javi se | Friendly ("get in touch") | Very common phrase |

## Positioning

For owner-run detailing studios and cleaning firms in Zagreb who lose jobs while their hands are
busy: **"Every inquiry gets a price and a free time in a minute, and you still confirm every
booking."**

Unlike booking platforms, it fits quote-based work and takes no commission. It is set up for you
locally, in Croatian.

## Homepage outline (HR first, EN version below the fold)

1. **Headline:** "Odgovor na svaki upit za minutu, a termin i dalje potvrđujete vi." Under it, a
   15-second demo video.
2. **The problem, in their words:** quotes from discovery calls, used only with permission.
3. **How it works:** 3 steps (inquiry → estimate and free times → you confirm).
4. **What it costs:** a pilot at €149, then two plans. No commission per booking.
5. **What it doesn't do:** it doesn't book without you, doesn't send spam, and doesn't keep data
   longer than agreed.
6. **FAQ:**
   - "Do my customers have to install anything?"
   - "What if the price is wrong?"
   - "Can I pause it?"
   - "Where is the data stored?"
   - "Is it AI?" (Mostly plain rules; anything unclear goes to you.)
7. **Contact:** a form that only collects what's needed, and a link to the privacy notice.
