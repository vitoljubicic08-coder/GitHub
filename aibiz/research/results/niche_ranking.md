## The 13 criteria per niche

### Cleaning companies

- **Number and accessibility:** Dozens of small firms in Zagreb (estimate); most advertise a phone, WhatsApp or form
- **Admin problems:** Every job needs a quote (m2, type, extras); staff scheduling; repeat customers rebook by phone
- **Typical inquiries:** Price for a 70 m2 deep clean, after-renovation cleaning, regular weekly cleaning, free dates
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Mostly phone and messaging (to confirm); field-service tools such as Jobber exist (reported at about $29-199/month)
- **Automation already used:** Little visible (to confirm)
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Unknown; regular customers are worth a lot over a year (to test)
- **GDPR complexity:** Low to medium: home addresses, key or access details
- **Implementation difficulty:** Medium: quote rules, team as the resource, travel time
- **Competition:** Low to medium locally
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium: reachable online
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 49-129 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Low

### Car detailing

- **Number and accessibility:** Dozens in the Zagreb area (estimate; the prospect scan counts them). Most show prices, Instagram and WhatsApp publicly
- **Admin problems:** Owner is hands-on and cannot answer while working; every quote depends on car size and condition; multi-hour jobs block the bay
- **Typical inquiries:** Price of polishing or ceramic coating for my car, free days, how long it takes
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Mostly phone, WhatsApp, Instagram and contact forms (to confirm in the prospect scan)
- **Automation already used:** Little visible (to confirm)
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Unknown. Job values on public price lists are high enough that one recovered job a month could cover a EUR 50-100 fee (to test)
- **GDPR complexity:** Low: name, phone, car model
- **Implementation difficulty:** Medium: price ranges, multi-hour slots, one resource (bay)
- **Competition:** Low to medium locally; generic tools exist but are not tailored
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium: owners are busy, but reachable on Instagram or in person; small market per city
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 49-99 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Low

### Small service companies and trades

- **Number and accessibility:** Many plumbers, electricians, HVAC and appliance-repair firms; less active online
- **Admin problems:** Missed calls while on a job, quotes, scheduling site visits around travel
- **Typical inquiries:** Can you come today, price for a boiler service, emergency call-out
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Phone and paper; some field-service apps
- **Automation already used:** Low (to confirm)
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Unknown; one missed job is worth EUR 50-300 (estimate)
- **GDPR complexity:** Low: name, address, phone
- **Implementation difficulty:** Medium: variable durations and travel
- **Competition:** Low to medium locally
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium to hard: owners are on site and less online
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 49-99 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Low

### Real-estate agencies

- **Number and accessibility:** Hundreds of agencies in Zagreb (estimate); listed on portals
- **Admin problems:** Many portal inquiries, slow replies, scheduling viewings, unqualified leads
- **Typical inquiries:** Is it still available, viewing times, price negotiation, financing
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Agency CRMs and portal tools
- **Automation already used:** Some (portal auto-replies, CRM)
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Unknown; one sale is worth thousands in commission
- **GDPR complexity:** Medium: buyers' financial situation and identity documents
- **Implementation difficulty:** Medium: portal integrations are limited
- **Competition:** Medium
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 100-250 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Medium

### Accounting firms

- **Number and accessibility:** Many small bookkeeping services in Zagreb (estimate)
- **Admin problems:** Chasing clients for documents every month, onboarding, deadline reminders; e-invoicing changes (Fiskalizacija 2.0) in 2026-2027
- **Typical inquiries:** Did you get my invoices, what do I need to send, deadlines
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Accounting packages and e-invoicing services
- **Automation already used:** Medium and growing with e-invoicing
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Unknown; time savings are clear
- **GDPR complexity:** High: clients' financial data (the brief excludes financial data from the first MVP)
- **Implementation difficulty:** High: document processing must be accurate
- **Competition:** Medium (accounting-software vendors add these features)
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium: trust-based
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 80-200 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Medium to high

### Gyms and fitness studios

- **Number and accessibility:** Dozens to low hundreds in Zagreb (estimate); active on Instagram
- **Admin problems:** Trial sign-ups not followed up, class booking, membership renewals
- **Typical inquiries:** Prices, trial session, class timetable, personal trainer availability
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Gym-management and class-booking apps (international and local)
- **Automation already used:** Common for class booking and payments
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Unknown; lead follow-up has clear value per new member
- **GDPR complexity:** Low to medium (health questionnaires are health data)
- **Implementation difficulty:** Medium
- **Competition:** High
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 50-150 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Medium

### Hair salons and barbershops

- **Number and accessibility:** Many; the national hairdressers' and beauticians' guild grew by 1,040 crafts (+10.7%) in 2025. Easy to list from Fresha/Zoyya directories and Instagram
- **Admin problems:** Phone bookings while cutting hair, no-shows, last-minute gaps
- **Typical inquiries:** Free slot today/tomorrow, prices, cancellations
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Fresha, Booksy, Zoyya (Croatian), Treatwell: cheap or free booking with reminders
- **Automation already used:** Yes: online booking and reminders are standard; Treatwell launched an AI receptionist in Sept 2026
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Anchors only: Fresha $19.95/month solo, Booksy GBP 40/month, AI receptionists EUR 59-199 (vendor listings)
- **GDPR complexity:** Low (contact and appointment data)
- **Implementation difficulty:** Low to build, but the booking platforms own the calendar; little open API access (assumption)
- **Competition:** Very high: platforms bundle what we would sell
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Easy to reach in person, hard to sell: most already have a tool
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 30-80 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Low to medium

### Tutors and education

- **Admin problems:** Scheduling lessons, payment reminders, parents' questions
- **Typical inquiries:** Free slots, price per hour, subjects, online or in person
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Calendars, booking links, school-management apps

- **GDPR complexity:** Medium: many clients are minors
- **Implementation difficulty:** Low
- **Competition:** Medium (free tools)
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 20-60 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Low

### Beauty salons (nails, lashes, cosmetics)

- **Number and accessibility:** Many; same guild and directories as hair salons
- **Admin problems:** Long appointments, deposits against no-shows, DMs with photos
- **Typical inquiries:** Price of a treatment, availability, aftercare questions
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Same platforms as hair salons (Fresha, Booksy, Zoyya, Treatwell)
- **Automation already used:** Yes, through the platforms
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Same anchors as hair salons
- **GDPR complexity:** Low to medium: skin conditions or allergies can be health data
- **Implementation difficulty:** Low to build; platform lock-in as above
- **Competition:** Very high
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Reachable via Instagram; already served
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 30-80 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Low to medium

### E-commerce shops

- **Number and accessibility:** Many, reachable online; not tied to Zagreb
- **Admin problems:** Order status questions, returns, product questions, abandoned carts
- **Typical inquiries:** Where is my order, return policy, size or stock questions
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Shop platforms with app stores full of AI support tools
- **Automation already used:** High
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Proven for tools, but crowded
- **GDPR complexity:** Low to medium
- **Implementation difficulty:** Medium
- **Competition:** Very high, international
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Hard: competing with global SaaS marketing
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 50-200 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Medium

### Hotels and accommodation

- **Number and accessibility:** Hundreds of private apartments in Zagreb, many more on the coast (no official Zagreb count found)
- **Admin problems:** Repeated guest questions, check-in instructions, guest registration, reviews
- **Typical inquiries:** Parking, check-in time, Wi-Fi, directions, late arrival
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Channel managers and property systems with automated messaging (e.g. Rentlio, Croatian; Smoobu)
- **Automation already used:** Common
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Anchored by channel-manager prices
- **GDPR complexity:** Medium: guest identity documents for registration
- **Implementation difficulty:** Medium
- **Competition:** High
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium (seasonal on the coast)
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 20-60 per property (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Medium

### Restaurants

- **Number and accessibility:** Many; easy to find
- **Admin problems:** Reservation calls at peak times, group bookings, menu questions
- **Typical inquiries:** Table tonight, group menu, opening hours, allergens
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Reservation platforms and Google reservations
- **Automation already used:** Common in larger venues
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Low margins; anchored by reservation platforms
- **GDPR complexity:** Low (allergen questions can touch health data)
- **Implementation difficulty:** Medium: table logic, high message volume
- **Competition:** High
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Medium: owners busy at service times
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 30-80 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** Medium (volume)

### Dental clinics

- **Number and accessibility:** Hundreds of private practices in Zagreb (estimate); reception staff act as gatekeepers
- **Admin problems:** Phone-heavy reception, recall reminders, treatment quotes, dental-tourism inquiries in English
- **Typical inquiries:** Pain/emergency slots, prices of implants or crowns, HZZO coverage
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Practice-management systems with patient records; HZZO-contracted practices also use the national eHealth system
- **Automation already used:** Some (SMS reminders in practice software); depth unknown
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Higher value per patient; no public evidence of what clinics pay for add-ons
- **GDPR complexity:** High: health data is a special category (GDPR Art. 9); excluded from the first MVP
- **Implementation difficulty:** High: integration with clinical systems and strict data handling
- **Competition:** Medium: specialised vendors
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Hard: formal buying, trust needed
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 100-300 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** High (compliance, accuracy)

### Private medical practices

- **Number and accessibility:** Many polyclinics and specialist practices; gatekept
- **Admin problems:** Appointment triage, referrals, results questions
- **Typical inquiries:** Which specialist, waiting time, price lists, referral rules
- **Time on repetitive tasks:** unknown: measure in interviews (minutes per day on calls/DMs)
- **Existing software:** Medical practice systems; national eHealth for HZZO work
- **Automation already used:** Some reminders; triage must stay human
- **Willingness to pay (evidence):** Unknown; high stakes
- **GDPR complexity:** Very high: health data, possible medical-device rules if it gives advice
- **Implementation difficulty:** High
- **Competition:** Medium
- **Acquisition difficulty:** Hard
- **Potential MRR per client (estimate):** EUR 100-300 (estimate)
- **Maintenance and support:** High
