# Demo script, onboarding, feedback and referrals

## Demo (20 minutes, on the client's own prices)

Before the meeting, copy `mvp/config/demo_detailing.json` (or the cleaning one). Put in their
services, prices, hours and bays or teams, then run `check-config`.

| Minute | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Repeat back what they told you: inquiries per week, slow replies, how they quote. Ask "did I get that right?" |
| 3–8 | **Customer view, on your phone:** type a real-sounding inquiry such as "koliko košta poliranje za SUV, subota ujutro?". Show the estimate, the free times and the hold, then enter a name and phone. Point at the **"NOT CONFIRMED YET"** text. |
| 8–12 | **Owner view, on the laptop:** the request appears under "Treba vašu potvrdu". Confirm it. Then show the reminder queued only because the customer said yes, the **pause** button, and blocking a day off. |
| 12–15 | The edge cases: a complaint goes straight to them; an impossible date gets asked again; a taken time offers new options. |
| 15–20 | **Ask, don't pitch.** "Would this have helped last week? What's missing? Would you try it for 30 days for €149?" Write down the exact words. |

Don't promise integrations that aren't built. WhatsApp sending, Google Calendar sync and payments
are "possible later, not in the pilot".

## Onboarding checklist (target: 5 working days, ≤4 hours of your time)

1. **Paperwork:**
   - pilot invoice paid;
   - data-processing agreement signed;
   - the client's privacy notice updated (we provide a draft paragraph).
2. **Intake form** (client fills it in, or you fill it in on a call):
   - services, with names in HR and EN;
   - prices per size or m², with the range they accept (± %);
   - job durations;
   - bays or teams, and which services each can do;
   - opening hours, closed days, minimum notice, buffer between jobs;
   - the owner's notification e-mail;
   - words customers actually use, for the keywords.
3. **Configuration:** write the client JSON, `check-config`, and run 10 test conversations,
   including 3 edge cases.
4. **Client test:** the client runs 5 test inquiries on their phone. Fix the wording.
5. **Go live:**
   - add the link to the Instagram bio, Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business away message;
   - pin a story;
   - set an `.ics` subscription in the owner's phone calendar.
6. **Day 3 and day 7 check-ins:** read every conversation with the client. Add missing keywords.
7. **Day 30 review:** the measures from the validation plan. Decide: continue on a plan, or stop.

## Client feedback (monthly, 5 minutes)

1. What went wrong this month (wrong price, wrong time, confusing message)?
2. One thing you'd add or remove.
3. How likely are you to recommend this to another business owner (0–10)?
4. Did any job come through the assistant that you think you'd otherwise have missed?

Log the answers in the CRM. A 0–6 answer to question 3 gets a call within a week.

## Referral process

- **When to ask:** only after a client has said the assistant was worth it, typically at the day-30
  review. Ask: "Do you know another owner with the same problem?"
- **Reward:** one month free for the referring client when the referred business pays its first
  monthly fee. Disclose it to the referred business.
- **Reviews:** never offer rewards for reviews, and never write or post reviews or testimonials
  for anyone. Quote a client publicly only with their written permission and their exact words.
