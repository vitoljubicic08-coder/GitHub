# Outreach kit (Croatian first, English below)

## Rules before sending anything

- **You send every message yourself**, one at a time, after reading the business's page. Nothing is
  automated and nothing goes out in bulk. Aim for 3–5 a day.
- **Channels, from most to least appropriate:**
  1. Visiting in person with the one-pager.
  2. A phone call by you to the business number.
  3. E-mail, or an Instagram or WhatsApp message, to a company (d.o.o. or j.d.o.o.).
- **What ZEK Art. 50 says** (NN 76/22, read on 19 Nov 2026):
  - Para. 1 requires **prior consent** for direct marketing by e-mail, SMS/MMS or automated calls.
  - Para. 4 exempts communication to **legal persons**.
  - Para. 3 always requires your real identity and a free way to opt out.
- **For an *obrt* (a natural person):** DMs and e-mails are very likely covered by para. 1. Visit
  or call, and send written material only after they say yes. A call you make yourself is not an
  "automated calling system". *Have HAKOM or a lawyer confirm this reading before a larger campaign.*
- **Opt-outs:** every written message carries one. If someone says no, mark them in the CRM and
  never contact them again.
- **Follow-up:** at most one, 4–6 days later. Then stop.
- **No fake claims:** no testimonials or "clients" until they exist. Say plainly that you are
  starting and looking for the first pilot businesses.
- **Personalise:** fill `[…]` with something true from their own page. If you can't find anything
  true to say, don't send.

## HR: Prvi e-mail (tvrtke)

**Naslov:** Upiti za [poliranje / generalno čišćenje] – kratko pitanje

Poštovani,

ja sam [Ime], iz Zagreba. Izrađujem jednostavnog asistenta za tvrtke koje cijenu daju prema
veličini posla, kao što je vaša. Vidio sam da na stranici [konkretno: objavljujete cijene po m² /
tražite da auto prvo dovezu na procjenu].

Asistent kupcu u minuti postavi dva-tri pitanja (usluga, veličina, željeni dan). Zatim mu da
**okvirnu cijenu iz vašeg cjenika** i ponudi **stvarno slobodne termine**. Vi svaki termin
potvrđujete jednim klikom, pa ništa nije rezervirano bez vas.

Tražim 2–3 tvrtke u Zagrebu za plaćeni pilot od 30 dana. Ne tražim da odmah išta kupite, nego
20 minuta razgovora: koliko upita dobivate i koliko vam vremena odlazi na odgovaranje.

Odgovara li vam kratak poziv ovaj ili sljedeći tjedan?

Lijep pozdrav,
[Ime Prezime] · [telefon] · [web ili LinkedIn]

*Ako ne želite ovakve poruke, odgovorite s „ne” i više vam se neću javljati.*

## HR: Kratka poruka (Instagram / WhatsApp Business)

Pozdrav! Vidim da radite [keramičku zaštitu / čišćenje nakon renovacije], super radovi na
[konkretno]. Radim mali alat koji kupcu iz Instagrama da okvirnu cijenu iz vašeg cjenika i ponudi
slobodan termin, a vi ga potvrdite. Smijem li vam poslati 1-minutni video kako izgleda? Ako ne,
nema problema.

## HR: Follow-up (jednom, nakon 4–6 dana)

Pozdrav, samo kratko provjeravam jeste li vidjeli moju poruku od [dan]. Ako vam trenutno ne treba,
slobodno recite i neću se više javljati. Ako vam je zanimljivo, mogu doći na 15 minuta i pokazati
na vašim cijenama.

## HR: Telefonski poziv (60–90 sekundi)

1. **Predstavljanje:** „Dobar dan, [Ime] ovdje, iz Zagreba. Imate li 2 minute ili da nazovem kasnije?”
2. **Razlog poziva:** „Razgovaram s [detailing studijima / servisima za čišćenje] o tome kako
   stižu odgovarati na upite dok rade. Smijem li pitati kako vi to rješavate?”
3. **Slušajte, zatim jedno pitanje:** „Koliko upita tjedno odgovorite tek sutradan ili uopće ne
   stignete?”
4. **Ako ima problem:** „Napravio sam asistenta koji kupcu da okvirnu cijenu iz vašeg cjenika i
   slobodne termine, a vi potvrđujete. Mogu li vam ga pokazati 15 minuta, uživo ili preko videa?”
5. **Ako nema problem:** „Hvala, to mi puno pomaže. Mogu li vas za mjesec dana pitati za mišljenje?”
   Zapišite odgovor u CRM.

## HR: Poziv na demonstraciju

Hvala na razgovoru! Kako smo dogovorili, [dan] u [sat] pokazat ću vam asistenta na vašim uslugama:
[usluga 1, usluga 2]. Bilo bi dobro da imate okvirni cjenik i radno vrijeme, a ja ću ih unijeti
prije sastanka. Traje 20 minuta. Ako vam termin ne odgovara, javite drugi.

## HR: Pitanja za razgovor (discovery)

1. Kako vam kupci najčešće šalju upit? (telefon, WhatsApp, Instagram, obrazac, e-mail, portal)
2. Koliko upita dobijete u prosječnom tjednu? A u sezoni?
3. Tko odgovara i kada? Što se dogodi s upitom dok radite?
4. Koliko upita tjedno ostane neodgovoreno isti dan?
5. Sjećate li se posla koji ste izgubili jer niste stigli odgovoriti ili poslati ponudu?
6. Kako danas određujete cijenu? Biste li dopustili da kupac vidi okvirni raspon prije pregleda?
7. Gdje vodite termine? (bilježnica, Google kalendar, aplikacija)
8. Koje alate plaćate mjesečno i koliko? Što vam se na njima ne sviđa?
9. Koliko vam vremena tjedno odlazi na dogovaranje termina i podsjećanje kupaca?
10. Što bi se moralo dogoditi u 30 dana da kažete da se isplatilo?
11. Tko u tvrtki odlučuje o ovakvim alatima?
12. Ako bi pilot od 30 dana koštao 149 €, što bi vas spriječilo da krenete?

## EN: First e-mail (companies)

**Subject:** Inquiries for [polishing / deep cleaning], a quick question

Hello,

I'm [Name], based in Zagreb. I'm building a simple assistant for businesses that price by the size
of the job, like yours. I saw on your site that you [specific: publish prices per m² / ask
customers to bring the car in for an assessment].

In about a minute it asks the customer two or three questions (service, size, preferred day).
It then gives them an **estimated price range from your own price list** and offers **real free
times**. You confirm each booking with one tap, so nothing is booked without you.

I'm looking for 2–3 Zagreb businesses for a paid 30-day pilot. I'm not asking you to buy anything
now, just for a 20-minute conversation: how many inquiries you get and how much time answering
them takes.

Would a short call this week or next suit you?

Best regards,
[Name Surname] · [phone] · [website or LinkedIn]

*If you'd rather not receive messages like this, reply "no" and I won't contact you again.*

## EN: Short message (Instagram / WhatsApp Business)

Hi! I see you do [ceramic coating / after-renovation cleaning], great work on [specific]. I'm
building a small tool that gives customers from Instagram an estimate from your own price list
and offers a free time, which you then confirm. May I send you a 1-minute video of it? If not, no
problem at all.

## EN: Follow-up (once, after 4–6 days)

Hi, just checking whether you saw my message from [day]. If it's not for you right now, just say
so and I won't write again. If it sounds useful, I can come by for 15 minutes and show it with
your prices.

## EN: Phone script (60–90 seconds)

1. **Introduce yourself:** "Hello, this is [Name] from Zagreb. Do you have two minutes, or should
   I call back later?"
2. **Reason for the call:** "I'm talking to [detailing studios / cleaning companies] about how they
   keep up with inquiries while they're working. May I ask how you handle it?"
3. **Listen, then ask one question:** "How many inquiries a week do you only answer the next day,
   or not at all?"
4. **If they have the problem:** "I've built an assistant that gives customers an estimate from
   your price list and free times, and you confirm. Could I show you for 15 minutes, in person or
   by video?"
5. **If they don't:** "Thanks, that really helps. Could I ask your opinion again in a month?"
   Log the answer in the CRM.

## EN: Demo invitation

Thanks for the call! As agreed, on [day] at [time] I'll show you the assistant set up with your
services: [service 1, service 2]. If you can share a rough price list and your opening hours, I'll
put them in beforehand. It takes 20 minutes. If the time doesn't suit you, suggest another.

## EN: Discovery questions

1. How do customers usually contact you? (phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, form, e-mail, a portal)
2. How many inquiries do you get in an average week? In high season?
3. Who answers, and when? What happens to an inquiry while you're working?
4. How many inquiries a week go unanswered the same day?
5. Can you remember a job you lost because you couldn't reply or quote in time?
6. How do you price today? Would you let a customer see an estimated range before an inspection?
7. Where do you keep bookings? (notebook, Google Calendar, an app)
8. What tools do you pay for monthly, and how much? What don't you like about them?
9. How much time a week goes on arranging bookings and reminding customers?
10. What would need to happen in 30 days for you to say it was worth it?
11. Who decides on tools like this?
12. If a 30-day pilot cost €149, what would stop you from starting?
