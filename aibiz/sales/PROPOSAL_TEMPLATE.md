## EN

**Proposal: inquiry and booking assistant for [Company]**
[Date] · valid for 14 days

**What you told us:**

- You get about [X] inquiries a week, mostly via [channel].
- About [Y] a week go unanswered the same day.
- About [Z] hours a week go on arranging bookings.

**What you get:**

1. **A page for inquiries under your name**, for the link in your Instagram bio, Google profile
   and WhatsApp away message. It works in Croatian and English.
2. **An estimated price from your own price list.** It is always labelled as an estimate that you
   confirm.
3. **Real free times** from your hours, holidays and job lengths. No double bookings.
4. **Nothing is booked without you.** You confirm with one tap, and anything the assistant doesn't
   understand goes to you.
5. **A reminder the day before**, only to customers who agreed to it.
6. **A short monthly report:** inquiries, confirmed bookings, and time to first reply.

**30-day pilot: €149**, one-off. If you continue, it is credited against set-up.

**After the pilot:** Starter €199 set-up + €59/month; Pro €399 + €99/month (adds WhatsApp
Business Platform reminders from your number and a monthly report). No lock-in, 30 days' notice.

**Your data:** before we start we sign a data-processing agreement (GDPR Art. 28). Your customers'
data stays yours, is stored in the EU and is deleted on the agreed schedule.

**Next step:** confirm by e-mail. Then send your price list and opening hours, and I set it up
within 5 working days.
