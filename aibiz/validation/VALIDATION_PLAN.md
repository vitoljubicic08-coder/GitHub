# Validation plan: will owners pay before we build more?

The MVP exists only to make conversations concrete. **Do not write more product code until the
gates below say continue.**

## Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Evidence that would confirm it | Evidence that would kill it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Owners of detailing studios and cleaning firms lose jobs or time because inquiries wait for answers and quotes | They name a number, e.g. "5–10 inquiries a week I answer the next day", or describe a lost job | "I answer everything within minutes" or "I have more work than I can take" |
| H2 | They would let customers see an **estimated** price range | They will write down their own price rules during the call | "Every job is different, I never give prices before I see it" (from most owners) |
| H3 | Customers would use a link instead of chatting | In a pilot, ≥30% of new inquiries arrive through the link within 4 weeks | <10% after 4 weeks with the link in bio, Google profile and WhatsApp away message |
| H4 | They pay €50–100/month after a paid pilot | A paid pilot (€149) and then a signed monthly plan | Interest only when free; "call me in spring" |

## Funnel targets (planning targets, not expected conversion rates)

| Stage | Target | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified businesses contacted | 30 (15 detailing, 15 cleaning) | Personal contact only, at most 3–5 a day. No bulk sending. |
| Meaningful conversations | 5 | 10+ minutes, discovery questions answered |
| Demos | 2 | The MVP shown on their own services and prices |
| Paid pilot | 1 | €149 for set-up + 30 days, credited against set-up if they continue |

## Decision gates

| When | Continue if | Change niche or offer if | Stop if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| After 30 contacts (target: day 30) | ≥5 conversations and ≥1 paid pilot | 3–4 conversations, no pilot, but pain confirmed (H1): rerun the 30 with the other top niche, or offer "quote follow-up only" | ≤2 conversations, or most say H1 is false |
| After the pilot's 30 days (target: day 60) | The pilot pays monthly. The assistant handled ≥15 inquiries with zero wrong bookings. The owner says it saved time or won a job. | The owner likes it but won't pay the price: test Starter at €39 or a per-booking fee with 10 more prospects | Under 5 inquiries through it in 30 days, or the owner turned it off |
| Day 90 | ≥3 paying clients, ≥€150 MRR, churn 0–1 | 1–2 paying clients: keep for income, but test one alternative from the comparison (e.g. websites + booking for trades) | 0 paying clients: stop the agency; reuse the code as a portfolio piece |

**Kill criteria are binding.** Write the numbers in the CRM every Friday. The decision is made from
the sheet, not from enthusiasm.

## Pilot design

- **Scope:** one business, one channel (a link in Instagram bio, Google Business Profile and the
  WhatsApp Business app away message), owner confirmation on.
- **Price:** €149 one-off for 30 days. It is credited against set-up if they continue on a plan.
- **Data:** the owner's own price rules and hours. A data-processing agreement must be signed
  before any real customer data is stored (see the legal section of the report).
- **Measures:**
  - inquiries handled;
  - share arriving through the link;
  - time from inquiry to first answer, before and after;
  - requests confirmed;
  - wrong or disputed bookings (target 0);
  - the owner's time estimate;
  - jobs the owner says came via the assistant.
- **Weekly 15-minute check-in:** what was wrong, what to change.

## Prospect list method (what we record, and what we don't)

Use **public business information only**: the business's own website, its Google Business
Profile and public directory listings. Record per business:

- name;
- niche;
- area;
- website;
- which public contact channels exist (form, phone, WhatsApp, e-mail, Instagram);
- whether a booking system is visible;
- what the site publicly states (published prices, "inspection first" and similar);
- the opportunity we see;
- priority;
- the source.

Do **not** copy personal mobile numbers or personal e-mail addresses into shared files; link to the
contact page instead. Never guess facts about a business. Write "to check" instead.

The real list is kept in the private report (tab "Prospects"), not in this public repository.
`prospects_template.csv` has the columns.
